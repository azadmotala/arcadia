Family boards

Your own leaderboard

Make a board, send the link, and everyone's scores land on the same weekly top 10, in every game, from any device. Last week's winner gets a crown.

  1. Name it. Anything you like. A few random letters go on the end so nobody can guess it.
  2. Send the link. Drop it in the family WhatsApp group, or text it to your friends.
  3. Play. Make the week's top 10 and your name goes up. New week every Monday.
