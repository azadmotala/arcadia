Space Cats

Mice in flying saucers, rat knights and bats are marching down. Five cats are all that stand in their way.

Pick your cat and shelter behind the moon rocks. Score 2,000 points and the fourth cat joins you. Reach 6,000 and the fifth one does.

    • Plays on phones, tablets and computers
    • Arrow keys and Space, or drag and hold
    • Five cats, five lives

    Family boards

    Your own leaderboard

    Make a board for your family or friends and send everyone the link. Their best scores land on the same top 10, from any phone, tablet or computer.

    1. Name it. Anything you like. A few random letters go on the end so nobody can guess it.
    2. Send the link. Drop it in the family WhatsApp group, or text it to your friends.
    3. Play. Every score that makes the top 10 goes on the board with your initials.

    This device is on the board . Play, and your best scores go on it.
